# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** a US Muslim donor deciding where to send zakat or sadaqah. They want evidence of impact, governance quality, and zakat eligibility before they give. Giving clusters around Ramadan and year-end.
- **Households:** families planning their giving together (shared giving plans, Family Giving Night, a shared shortlist). A first-class user, not an add-on.

## Job

Pick charities with confidence, then plan and record the giving: browse and compare evaluated charities, read why each one scored as it did, check whether it can take zakat, and track intended and confirmed donations.

## Position

GMG combines evidence-based charity evaluation with a per-charity zakat judgment in one place. The GMG score blends impact, alignment and risk; zakat eligibility is assessed for each charity. Charity Navigator does not judge zakat, and Islamic charity directories do not evaluate impact.

## Constraints and product facts

- The GMG score is `amalScore` (impact + alignment − risk). Charity Navigator's rating is a different number and must never be presented as the GMG score.
- Guidance is informational, not religious or legal advice; binding zakat rulings belong to qualified scholars.
- Anonymous visitors see a baseline evaluation; the detailed analysis sits behind sign-in.
- Crawlable, server-rendered public pages matter: organic search is the main acquisition channel.

## Terminology

Open decision: fiqh terms (zakat, sadaqah, asnaf) and product terms (GMG score, wallet, DonorFit, rubric/edition versions) are judged case by case for plain language, not by a blanket rule.
